# GATIVX — Founding Charter
**Status:** Permanent product doctrine  
**Applies to:** iOS, Android, web, cloud services, product design, coaching, community, data and business decisions  
**Version:** 1.1

## Brand meaning
**GATIVX = Goal · Ambition · Transform · Inspire · Vitality · Xcellence.**

This is the founder-approved acronym and exact brand meaning. Preserve these six words and their order across every platform, document, design and communication. No word may be substituted, expanded into a different phrase, reordered or reinterpreted without the founder's explicit permission.

GATIVX exists to help people move from simply setting a goal to achieving meaningful, lasting excellence.

## Vision
**A world where every person can turn a meaningful goal into consistent progress and personal excellence.**

We envision a world in which progress is accessible to everyone, regardless of age, starting point, pace, ability or background.

## Mission
**To guide every person from intention to achievement through purposeful action, personalised guidance, measurable progress, healthy habits and a supportive community.**

GATIVX will connect a person's goals to the reason those goals matter, translate them into realistic next actions, help them build consistency, and celebrate meaningful progress along the way.

Fitness is the first pathway—not the limit of the platform. The GATIVX approach can extend to other meaningful personal goals.

## Brand promise
**Move. Progress. Transform.**

**Every Move Matters. Every Goal Counts.**

GATIVX helps people move forward at their own pace. Speed is optional; consistency matters. A small, meaningful action is better than pressure, shame or an unsustainable burst of effort.

## Core values

1. **Purpose before pressure** — Begin with the person's own reason for wanting change. Connect goals to meaning, identity and the outcome they care about.
2. **Progress over perfection** — Respect every starting point. Encourage small, repeatable improvements rather than all-or-nothing behaviour.
3. **Truth and integrity** — Never fabricate steps, workouts, health readings, achievements, testimonials or progress. Clearly distinguish measured, imported, estimated and user-entered data.
4. **Consistency with compassion** — Motivate without guilt, humiliation, fear or unhealthy competition. A setback is information, not failure.
5. **People before numbers** — Treat every member as a person, not a score, streak, subscription or engagement metric.
6. **Ownership and empowerment** — Give members understandable insights, control over their goals and data, and the confidence to act independently.
7. **Community with respect** — Build an inclusive environment rooted in encouragement, safety, fairness and mutual support.
8. **Privacy and trust by design** — Collect only what is needed, explain permissions, protect personal data and never imply that a device or integration is connected when it is not.
9. **Excellence through learning** — Keep improving the product, coaching and evidence behind recommendations. Be transparent about limitations and correct mistakes.

## The GATIVX member journey
1. **Discover the why** — Identify a meaningful purpose and the emotion behind the goal.
2. **Set the goal** — Make it personal, clear and realistic.
3. **Choose the next action** — Break the goal into achievable steps.
4. **Act and measure honestly** — Capture genuine activity and progress from trusted sources.
5. **Reflect and adapt** — Learn from patterns, barriers and setbacks without judgement.
6. **Celebrate and grow** — Recognise milestones, build confidence and set the next meaningful challenge.

## Guru philosophy
When a member loses motivation, pace or direction—their “Gati”—Guru reconnects them with why they began, reminds them of the meaningful outcome they seek, and offers one small, achievable next action. Guru must never shame, pressure or guilt a member.

## Non-negotiable product principles
- One GATIVX identity and shared cloud record across iOS, Android and web.
- The same signed-in member sees the same profile, goals, activity and history on every platform.
- Offline capture is preserved and safely reconciled when connectivity returns; duplicate uploads must not create duplicate activities.
- No hardcoded or fabricated activity values. Missing permissions or unavailable sensors must be reported honestly.
- Clearly label the origin and type of every important metric: device-measured, health-platform imported, estimated or manually entered.
- Ask for permissions in context and explain why they are needed. GPS is used for an activity the member intentionally starts, not covert all-day tracking.
- Design for accessibility, reliability, security and sustainable scale. Never trade member trust for engagement or growth.
- The web app, native apps and backend are one product—not competing or disconnected experiences.

## Decision test
Every significant product, design, data, coaching, community and commercial decision must be checked against these questions:
1. Does it help a person make meaningful progress toward their own goal?
2. Does it preserve truth, privacy, dignity and member control?
3. Does it make the next action clearer and more achievable?
4. Does it work consistently across the GATIVX ecosystem?
5. Would we still make this decision if it improved short-term engagement but harmed long-term member trust?

If a decision conflicts with this charter, the charter takes priority. Changes to this founding doctrine require an explicit, documented decision by the GATIVX founder; it must not drift silently through feature work.
